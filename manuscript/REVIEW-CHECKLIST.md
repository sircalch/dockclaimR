# Human review checklist before external circulation

This checklist is intentionally incomplete until the authors review it. Do not
submit the manuscript or create a DOI solely because these files exist.

- [ ] Confirm the final author order, affiliations, ORCID identifiers, and
      corresponding author.
- [ ] Record each author's CRediT contributions and obtain agreement from all
      authors.
- [ ] Independently rerun the complete workflow or inspect its retained logs,
      selection maps, manifests, and hashes.
- [ ] Decide whether the retained derived outputs can be archived legally and,
      if so, prepare a versioned data/software deposit with its licence.
- [ ] Choose a target journal only after checking scope, indexing, costs,
      language, article type, and data/code policies.
- [ ] Verify all citation metadata and bibliography style against that journal.
- [ ] Check every numerical value, table, caption, and interpretation against
      the generated outputs.
- [ ] Confirm that the manuscript makes no claim about affinity, binding,
      biological activity, efficacy, or virtual-screening performance.
- [ ] Run a similarity check and revise the prose with the authors' own
      scientific voice before submission.
